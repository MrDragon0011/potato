TAD Chat — Terms of Service

Last updated: September 8, 2026

TAD Chat ("the Service") is a free chat application created by Lawrence Tong for students of the class of 2031 to talk about coursework together. By entering a username, reading messages, or posting messages, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

The one rule that matters most: TAD Chat exists so you can discuss how to approach homework problems — understanding a concept, clarifying an assignment, checking that you read a question correctly, or working through where you are stuck. It is not a place to post, request, or copy completed answers, worked solutions, code, essays, or lab data that is meant to be your own work.

1. Purpose and acceptable use

You may use TAD Chat to:

  • Ask what an assignment is asking for or when it is due.
  • Discuss concepts, methods, and strategies for solving a type of problem.
  • Point a classmate toward a resource, section of the textbook, or idea.
  • Talk through where you got stuck without handing over the finished result.

You may not use TAD Chat to:

  • Post or ask for complete answers, full solutions, or work that is meant to be done individually.
  • Share answer keys, graded work, quiz or test contents, or material a teacher has said not to share.
  • Do another student's assignment for them, or ask someone to do yours.
  • Harass, threaten, impersonate, or bully anyone, or post hateful, obscene, or off-topic spam.
  • Break any school rule, or any local, state, or federal law.
